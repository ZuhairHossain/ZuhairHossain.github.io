Hello! I am **Syed Zuhair Hossain** (you can call me **Zuhair**) from **Dhaka, Bangladesh**. I am a passionate tech-enthusiast with a strong interest in Science and Arts. I am into learning new things about technology and research methodology with an engineering mindset. I completed my undergraduate degree in [Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)](https://www.bracu.ac.bd/academics/departments/computer-science-and-engineering) at [Brac University](https://www.bracu.ac.bd/) in May 2023.

I am currently a **DevOps Engineer** at [Therap (BD) Ltd.](https://therapbd.com/), where I work within the Application and Middleware (DevOps) Team. My role involves managing and maintaining the company's systems and infrastructure, while also applying DevOps principles to automate and streamline our software delivery processes.

Previously, from February 2023 to September 2025, I worked as a **DevOps Engineer** in the Platform Architecture and Operations Department of the ITP Division at [RedDot Digital IT Ltd. (subsidiary of Robi Axiata Ltd.)](https://www.reddotdigitalit.com/). I was responsible for managing the platform's core infrastructure, utilizing advanced automation tools like CI/CD, Docker, and Kubernetes.

I completed my undergraduate thesis on Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) under the guidance of [Dr. Jannatun Noor Mukta](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=nUColk4AAAAJ&hl=en) in collaboration with [Dr. A. B. M. Alim Al Islam](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=K-AIPzQAAAAJ&hl=en), [Dr. Tanjir Rashid Soron](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8it468kAAAAJ&hl=en), and [Dr. Md. Golam Zel Asmaul Husna](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=S4XSndkAAAAJ&hl=en). We were honored to receive a prestigious *research grant* from **Brac University**, which enabled us to expand our research work.
