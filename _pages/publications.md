    <div class="pub-title">Decoding Mental Friction: Understanding Social and Non-social Biases in LLMs Toward Autistic Users</div>
    <div class="pub-authors"><b>Syed Zuhair Hossain</b>, Valerie De Ryan, Katelyn Pryor, Shameem Ahmed</div>
    <div class="pub-venue">Submitted to Autism in Adulthood · 2026</div>
    <p class="card-excerpt" style="margin-top: 0.5rem;">A mixed-methods user study with autistic and neurotypical adults examining how LLMs like ChatGPT exhibit social and non-social biases toward neurodiverse users. Findings reveal that LLMs rely on medicalized generalizations, place communication burden on autistic users, and default to sensory-overloading text formats. Proposes actionable design implications including "Tone Toggles" and inquiry-first architectures for cognitive accessibility.</p>
